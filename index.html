Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding coverage, payments, and driving rules.

What documents are needed to pick up a car?

You need a valid driver's license held for at least one year, an active credit card in the primary renter's name, and a passport or government photo ID for international travelers.

Is a security deposit held during the rental?

We perform a temporary pre-authorization hold ($200 to $500 depending on vehicle tier) which releases automatically upon safe return and vehicle check.

Can I return the vehicle to a different location?

Yes, one-way trips are supported across our airport and downtown hubs. Any applicable relocation surcharge calculates transparently at checkout.

What is your electric vehicle charging policy?

Electric models include access to public fast-chargers. We simply ask to return the car with at least 20% battery remaining so our detailing crew can move the fleet.
