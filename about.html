Our Origins

Disrupting the Conventional Rental Model

Traditional luxury car rentals have long been defined by tedious airport queues, vague category placeholders, surprise security holds, and vehicle substitutions. Veloce was engineered to dismantle every single one of these frictions.

Founded in 2014 by seasoned motorsport engineers and private aviation directors, Veloce introduced real-time chassis reserving, mobile biometrics, and airside keyless unlock. Every car in our fleet is factory-calibrated, detailed to concours standards, and positioned precisely where your journey begins.
