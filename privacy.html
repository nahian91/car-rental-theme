Privacy Policy & Security

Last updated: October 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Airside PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

1. Information We Collect

Veloce Fleet Technologies Inc. ("Veloce", "we", "our") collects personal and biometric information required to facilitate secure vehicle handover, driver verification (KYC), and smartphone NFC key provisioning. This includes:

  • Government-issued driver's license numbers and state/country jurisdiction.
  • Encrypted facial biometrics used for 60-second remote identity verification prior to arrival.
  • Flight tail numbers, commercial arrival times, and FBO staging notes.
  • Tokenized payment credentials processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 secure gateways.

2. Use of Digital NFC Keys and Telemetry

When you unlock a Veloce vehicle using our digital mobile pass, our connected vehicle telematics log diagnostic handshakes, mileage counters, and battery/fuel levels solely to automate contactless checkouts and guarantee safety compliance.

3. Data Protection and Sharing

Your data is never sold or commercialized. We share verification parameters only with authorized FBO airside ground handling partners and secure payment clearinghouses strictly necessary to complete your expedition.

4. Client Rights & Deletion Requests

You retain the right to request a full audit or permanent purge of your KYC logs and saved payment tokens by contacting our data privacy officer at privacy@velocefleet.com.
